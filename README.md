# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis.
# With Q&A Performance Report

## 📋 Project Overview

This project provides a detailed data analysis of a Coffee Shop's retail sales to identify **actionable insights** for enhancing business performance, optimizing operations, and driving revenue growth.

The primary deliverable is a **Question & Answer Performance Report** generated from an underlying dashboard and extensive pivot table analysis, transforming raw sales metrics into clear business recommendations.

## 🎯 Goal of the Analysis

To answer key business intelligence questions regarding sales patterns, product performance, and location efficiency, specifically:
* Identifying **peak sales hours** and busiest days of the week.
* Tracking **monthly revenue growth** and overall sales trends.
* Benchmarking performance across different **store locations**.
* Determining the **Average Price/Order (Average Bill)**.
* Pinpointing the **best-selling products** by revenue and category.

---

## 💡 Key Findings & Business Insights

The analysis of the sales data revealed several critical insights:

| Business Area | Key Finding | Actionable Insight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Peak Sales Time** | The critical rush hour window is concentrated between **8 AM and 10 AM**, with **10 AM** being the absolute peak for volume. | Maximize staffing, machine availability, and pre-prep inventory during this 2-hour window. |
| **Daily/Weekly Sales** | **Monday** is the highest revenue day, and **Friday** has the highest customer traffic. Weekend sales are the weakest overall. | Target weekend customers with specific promotions (e.g., brunch specials or happy hour) to boost Saturday revenue. |
| **Growth Trend** | Sales show a strong, consistent **upward trend**, increasing over 118% from the lowest month (February) to the highest (June). | Analyze and replicate the successful strategies from the May-June period across all months. |
| **Customer Spend** | The **Average Price/Order is $4.69**. | Train staff to focus on simple upselling (e.g., adding a bakery item or a flavor shot) to push the average order value past the $5.00 mark. |
| **Product Dominance** | **Coffee and Tea** products drive nearly **67%** of all total revenue. **Barista Espresso** is the single highest-revenue product type. | Prioritize the quality and promotion of core coffee and espresso drinks, as they are the main profit drivers. |

## 📖 How to Read This Report

This analysis is structured as a direct response to the business questions outlined in the project brief. Stakeholders should focus on the **"Actionable Insight"** column for quick, high-impact business decisions.

* **For Operations Managers:** Focus on the **Hourly Peak Times** to adjust staffing and shift schedules for maximum efficiency and speed during the 8 AM - 10 AM window.
* **For Marketing Directors:** Focus on the **Daily/Weekly Sales** and **Product Dominance** to plan promotions. For instance, run a loyalty program for **Barista Espresso** on Saturdays to increase low weekend traffic.
* **For Executives/Owners:** Focus on the **Growth Trend** and **Store Performance** to assess the overall health of the business and identify areas for potential investment or expansion.
